Correction to this session's earlier claims

WISE beats INLAY on Qwen2.5-7B CounterFact: 0.9466 vs 0.8944. Every proposal, message, and chart built earlier in this session says INLAY "leads every method tested." That's true on GPT-J and Mistral, and true on zsRE everywhere, but not true on Qwen CounterFact specifically. This needs fixing in the outreach materials before anything is sent.

Metric mismatch, not a real result

MEMIT and AlphaEdit's Qwen CounterFact numbers below (0.88, 0.72) were measured with a different metric (probability-of-success) than INLAY/ROME/WISE/GRACE's (token-accuracy harmonic mean). They are not directly comparable to INLAY's 0.8944 even though they look similar in magnitude. No apples-to-apples score exists for MEMIT/AlphaEdit on Qwen CounterFact.
